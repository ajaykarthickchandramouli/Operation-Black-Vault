Case 007: The Missing Bhusanam

Perumal's Bhushanam were stolen from the temple. They're very costly and belong to Perumal. We have to get them in 5 minutes for an Utsavam. We know the location. But they are kept in a vault which has a code to be accessed. The thief claimed that we had possessed all the clues throughout the investigation. Use them to find the jewels, Sherlock. All the best!

Suspect: In Custody
Evidence: Under Analysis
Vault: Locked

Time Remaining: 05:00




The following questions were given by the thief. Answer them, arrange the answers in the same order and enter it to open the vault!


E-01 - The Nature of the Supreme
Count what the record leaves absent. The Supreme is described through boundless virtues, yet the space reserved for the contrary remains untouched.
E-02 - The Pilgrim's Origin
Upon an island embraced by a sacred river, He rests with His head towards the forbidden direction. Yet, no injunction binds Him.
E-03 — The Unspoken Foundation
A seeker approaches the final refuge, carrying neither the pride of personal effort nor the claim of independent agency. The ancient record describes the conditions accompanying this act of surrender. Examine the doctrine to which the record alludes.
E-04 — The Marks of the Tradition
Seek the wisdom preserved by the Acharyas, and the insignia of renunciation. Though they belong to different contexts, both bear a common mark. Discover it.
